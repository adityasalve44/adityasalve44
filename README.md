### 🛍️ Wingo — Hyperlocal Commerce & Delivery Platform

<p align="center">

</div>

<table align="center" width="100%">
<tr><th align="left">System</th><th align="left">Capabilities</th></tr>

<tr><td>🚴 Delivery</td><td>Agent assignment, OTP-based delivery confirmation, earnings tracking</td></tr>

<tr><td>🔔 Notifications</td><td>Full push-notification engine (QStash + VAPID) across business events, with idempotency</td></tr>
